Agreement with labels and validity are unrelated &
Inspection-report strictness: the version with the highest label agreement
correlates with observed strictness at $0.07$; the prose version at $0.43$.
Across eleven instrument families the best-agreeing version was usually the
worse measure. \\
\addlinespace[3pt]
Facts outperform judgements &
The one instrument family whose out-of-sample agreement improved across
versions did so when judgement questions were replaced by factual ones.
Judgement questions track the model's own generosity rather than the
document. \\
\addlinespace[3pt]
Separate calls can manufacture independence &
Enacted warmth and strictness correlate at $0.59$ in the visits. The
single-call prose instrument gives $0.54$; instruments scoring
each construct in a separate call return $0.08$ to $0.22$. \\
\addlinespace[3pt]
The model is part of the instrument &
An unchanged rubric moved from one scoring model to another fell from
$\kappa_w = 0.63$ to $0.12$. Every instrument is pinned to the model it was
tested with; one (faith prominence) is kept on an older model for that reason. \\
