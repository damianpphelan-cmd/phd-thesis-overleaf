\textit{Notes:} Column~(1) reproduces the reduced-form estimates from the full sample for reference. Column~(2) restricts the sample to students who do not take A-level economics. The IV design assumes that offering economics changes earnings only for the students who take it. If availability changed the earnings of non-takers, that assumption would fail. The test has a limit. When a school starts offering economics, the students who take it up leave the non-taker group, so the non-takers at offering and non-offering schools are slightly different sets of students.
