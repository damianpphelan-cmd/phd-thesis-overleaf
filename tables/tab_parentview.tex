\footnotesize\textit{Notes:} Standard errors in parentheses (HC3; the behaviour-policy row
clustered on the shared document digest). The document-instrument regressions control
for the national control set without the grade and exclude late-entry schools; Panel B
adds the predecessor-filled 2019 grade. The predicted-warmth and predicted-teaching
rows are correlations over every school with an out-of-sample prediction and a
survey (training schools excluded; no late-entry exclusion, since no regression is
run). They carry no controlled-$\beta$ column because the prediction models'
training targets were already residualised on the national control set, so a
controlled regression would partial the same variables twice; the demographics
row instead partials FSM, EAL and KS2 intake out of both the prediction and the
composite over the same sample. The teaching prediction correlates with the parent
composite almost as strongly as the warmth prediction, so the association reads as
report-tone validity shared across dimensions rather than a warmth-specific signal.
Parent View is a soft criterion: it conflates school culture with parent
satisfaction, each school contributes its single most recent survey release (releases span
September 2022 to September 2025), and responses are not temporally aligned with the
instrument sources. The document-based instruments (website, behaviour policy) do not
track parents' experienced climate; the report-derived predictions do, consistent with both
drawing on visits to the school. Panel B is endogenous --- parents plausibly respond to
results as well as climate --- so it is reported as a description, never as a causal
predictor.
